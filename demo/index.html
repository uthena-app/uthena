Questions

Frequently asked

What is PLR and can I really keep 100% of the revenue?

Private Label Rights means you can rebrand, edit, and resell the course under your own name. You pay once, sell as many times as you like, and keep every dollar. Uthena takes 0% of your resales.

How is MRR different from PLR?

Master Resell Rights lets your buyers also resell the course. PLR is for you to sell; MRR is for you to build a reseller chain on top. Some courses on Uthena offer both, and about 261 courses offer MRR.

Can I edit the videos and course materials?

Yes. Source files, scripts, slide decks and videos are all included and editable in standard tools (Premiere, Final Cut, PowerPoint, Google Slides). Translate the course, re-record with your own voice, change the instructor name — your call.

Do you offer refunds?

14-day return rights, no questions asked. Single courses are refundable within 14 days if the course has not been downloaded. Email support@uthena.com to start a refund.

What format are the courses delivered in?

MP4 video, PDF workbooks, slide decks (PPTX/Keynote), and editable project files where applicable. All downloads are signed, expiring URLs — audit-logged.

How do I become an instructor?

Apply through the Earn with Uthena page. We license original work from independent creators and split revenue 60/40 in your favor (60% to you). All courses must include PLR rights — it's the whole model.

How does the affiliate program work?

Sign up at affiliate.uthena.com. You'll get a personal mini-shop at uthena.com/yourhandle, plus affiliate links for any course. 20% commission on every sale attributed to your links (30-day cookie). Paid via PayPal on request.

Can I use the courses on Udemy, Skillshare, or other platforms?

No. PLR licenses explicitly prohibit resale on Udemy, Skillshare, or any other third-party course platform you don't own. You can sell on your own site, your own LMS, or bundle with your own products.
