# Data Sharing Opt-Out

Uthena respects your privacy choices. This page explains how to opt out of the sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising use of your personal information, and how to exercise the related rights granted to you by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## What "data sharing" means on Uthena

We share your data with the service providers that operate Uthena on our behalf: payment processing (Stripe), file storage and streaming (Bunny.net), authentication and database (Supabase), transactional email (Resend / Amazon SES), and analytics (Plausible or self-hosted PostHog). We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

## How to submit a request

You can use the request form on this page, email us directly, or sign in to your account and adjust the privacy controls under **Account → Settings → Privacy**. Pick whichever path is most convenient for you.

The form asks for:

- **First name** and **last name** — so we can identify the requester.
- **Email address** — the address on your Uthena account, or the one you would like to be associated with the request if you do not have an account.
- **Request type** — *Do Not Sell*, *Do Not Share*, *Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information*, or *Object to processing* (GDPR Art. 21).
- **Reason (optional)** — anything you would like us to know about the context of the request. This field is optional and helps us triage, but you do not need to fill it in.

Submitting the form does not require you to provide your password, payment-card details, government ID, or any other sensitive file. If anything in a request asks for those, it is a phishing attempt — please report it.

To make any of these requests by email instead, write to [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com). Include the email address on your account. We respond within the time required by law.

## Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) gives you the following rights over your personal information:

- **Right to Know** — request a copy of the personal information we have collected about you in the preceding 12 months, including the categories, sources, and business purposes of collection.
- **Right to Delete** — request that we delete your personal information, subject to the legal exceptions in CCPA §1798.105.
- **Right to Correct** — request that we correct inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information** — request that we only use your sensitive personal information for the limited purposes permitted by CCPA §1798.121 (e.g. providing the services you requested).
- **Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing** — direct us not to sell or share your personal information. Make that request here, on the **Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information** form below, or by sending the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal from your browser (see below).
- **Right to Non-Discrimination** — we will not deny service, charge a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised any of these rights.

The **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link on the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) lands here. To exercise the right, submit the form below or email us at [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com). We will respond to your request within **15 business days** as required by CCPA §1798.135.

## Your GDPR rights — Art. 21 right to object

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction where the GDPR applies, Article 21 of the GDPR gives you the right to object to processing of your personal information in two specific situations:

- **Objection to processing based on legitimate interests** (GDPR Art. 6 (1) (f)) — where we process your data on the basis of our "legitimate interests" (for example, security and fraud prevention, certain product-improvement analytics). We will either stop the processing or demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your objection.
- **Objection to direct marketing** — where we use your data for direct marketing purposes, you may object at any time and we will stop. We do not currently run any direct marketing that would qualify under this ground, but the right is preserved here for completeness and so that future integrations honour it from day one.

To exercise your right to object, use the *Object to processing* option on the form below, or email us at [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com). We will respond within **30 days** as required by GDPR Art. 12 (3).

## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

Our website honors the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal. When your browser sends the `Sec-GPC: 1` request header, we treat it as a valid opt-out of any sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising use of your personal information for the associated browser or device, exactly as required by the CCPA regulations (Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, §7025) and by California Assembly Bill 302 (AB-302, 2023). If we are able to associate the GPC-bearing device with a signed-in Uthena account, we apply the opt-out to that account as well.

GPC is recognized automatically — you do not need to submit the form below if your browser is sending the signal. To learn more about Global Privacy Control, see [https://globalprivacycontrol.org/](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/). Other than GPC, we do not recognise other "Do Not Track" signals that may be sent from your web browser or device.

## Other U.S. state privacy laws

Where local law provides additional rights (e.g. Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, Utah UCPA, Texas TDPSA, Oregon OCPA, Montana MCDPA, Iowa SF 262), we honor those rights as well. Submit any of these requests via the form below with *Object to processing* selected, or by emailing [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com), and we will respond in the time required by the law of your state.

## Identity verification

To protect your privacy, we verify that the person submitting the request is the data subject (or an authorized agent — see below). Verification depends on the sensitivity of the request:

- **Non-sensitive requests** (e.g. opt-out of sale/sharing, marketing opt-out) — we verify by matching the email address you submit against the email address on your Uthena account. If you do not have an account, we verify by sending a confirmation link to the email you provided before processing the request.
- **Sensitive requests** (e.g. deletion, full data export, sensitive-PI limitation) — we may ask for additional verification, such as a copy of a government-issued ID, before processing. We will tell you what we need before requesting it, and we will delete the verification artifact after we have completed the request.

We will never ask for your password, your full payment-card number, or sensitive files beyond what is strictly necessary to verify your identity. If anything in a verification request asks for those, it is a phishing attempt — please report it.

## Authorized agents

Under the CCPA, you may designate an **authorized agent** to submit a privacy request on your behalf. Before we accept a request from an agent, we require:

1. Proof that the agent is authorized to act on your behalf (a signed authorization letter, or a valid power of attorney under California Probate Code §4121).
2. Verification of the agent's identity.
3. Verification of **your** identity directly with us (so we know you actually authorized the request).

If you are an authorized agent submitting on behalf of someone else, please write to [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com) with both the authorization document and the data subject's contact information, and we will guide you through the verification process.

## Response windows

| Jurisdiction | Right exercised | Response window |
|---|---|---|
| California (CCPA / CPRA) | Opt-out of sale / sharing / targeted advertising | **15 business days** (CCPA §1798.135) |
| California (CCPA / CPRA) | Know / Delete / Correct | **45 days**, extendable by 45 days with notice |
| European Economic Area (GDPR Art. 12 (3)) | Objection (Art. 21) and most other rights | **30 days**, extendable by 60 days for complex requests |
| Virginia / Colorado / Connecticut / Utah / Texas / Oregon / Montana / Iowa | State-specific opt-outs and rights | As required by the applicable state statute (typically 30–45 days) |

If we cannot fulfil your request within the applicable window, we will notify you of the delay and the reason, and we will offer the appeals process required by your jurisdiction.

## What we do not need from you

We will never ask for your password, your full card number, or sensitive files (e.g. government IDs) to process a privacy request — beyond what is strictly necessary to verify your identity for sensitive requests, and only after telling you what we need. If anything in a request asks for those, it is a phishing attempt — please report it to [privacy@uthena.com](mailto:privacy@uthena.com).
