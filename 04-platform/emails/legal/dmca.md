# DMCA Notice-and-Takedown

Uthena respects intellectual property. If you believe content on Uthena infringes your copyright, you can send us a DMCA notice. We will review and act on valid notices as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512.

The **designated agent's contact info** (name, email, mailing address, phone) is rendered above this section from `platform_settings.dmca_agent` — editable by admins from `/admin/dmca-agent`.

## How to File a Notice

Your notice must include the six elements required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or an authorized agent.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works at one online location).
3. Identification of the infringing material with information sufficient for us to locate it (URL, product title, partner handle, etc.).
4. Your contact information: name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

## How to File a Counter-Notice

If you believe content removed under a DMCA notice was the result of a mistake or misidentification, you may send us a counter-notice under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3). The counter-notice must include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed or to which access was disabled, and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed.
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the address you provide (or, if you are outside the United States, any judicial district in which Uthena may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original notice or an agent of that person.
5. A statement that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original notice or an agent of that person.

After we receive a valid counter-notice, we forward it to the original claimant. The original claimant then has 10–14 business days to file a federal action. If no action is filed, we may restore the material at our discretion.

## What Happens After We Receive Your Notice

- We acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
- We review the notice and, if it is complete and facially valid, remove or disable access to the material.
- We notify the partner who posted the material and provide them with the counter-notice process.
- We log the takedown in our internal compliance system.

## Repeat Infringer Policy

Uthena terminates the accounts of partners and customers who are **repeat copyright infringers**. We track takedowns per partner and per customer; after a second valid takedown against the same account, the account is suspended and pending earnings are held while we review.

## Misuse Warning — 17 U.S.C. § 512(f)

Knowingly **false** statements in a DMCA notice or counter-notice are subject to **perjury prosecution** and may expose the sender to **damages** (including costs and attorneys' fees) under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f). Submitting a notice or counter-notice in bad faith, or without a good-faith belief in its truth, is a misuse of the DMCA process. We reserve the right to suspend the account of any user who submits a notice or counter-notice that we determine, in our sole discretion, to be knowingly false.

## Counter-Notice Contact

Send counter-notices to the **designated agent** listed above with the subject "Counter-notice" and all five required elements. The agent's email routes both DMCA notices and counter-notices — admins keep the contact up to date at `/admin/dmca-agent`.
