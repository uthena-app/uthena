question: "What am I allowed to do with a PLR course I bought?"
last_updated: 2025-09-08
---

A PLR license gives you broad rights to rebrand and resell the course as your own. Specifically, you can:

- Sell the course separately
- Bundle it with other products
- Use it as a bonus for another product
- Add it to paid membership sites
- Add your own bonuses to the Product for sale
- Sell it as a physical product or a digital product
- Put your own name on the sales letter
- Rename the product
- Edit the sales material
- Edit the content of the product
- Use the source code / material to create new products
- Use the name(s) of the author / creator / seller of the Product
- Translate the course into other languages
- Use it to build a list

You are **not allowed** to resell the course as-is on marketplaces where Uthena also lists the same product, or to claim authorship of the underlying content. See [prohibited PLR uses](/faq#prohibited-uses) for the full list.
