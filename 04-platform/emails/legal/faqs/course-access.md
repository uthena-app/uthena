### Following the course

For regular access, the course appears in your [Uthena library](/library) within seconds of payment confirmation — see [Delivery](/delivery) for the full instant-access policy. There you will find your course with all the materials and videos for you to follow.

If the course does not appear within a few minutes, please check your spam first. If after 10 minutes it is still missing, send us an email at [info@uthena.com](mailto:info@uthena.com).

### PLR / MRR access

If you buy a PLR/MRR license, you will receive both the access to the course, as well as a download link to download all the files related to it. Next to this, you will receive a license stating your details.

You will receive after the order:

- Access to our e-learning portal
- Download link for the source files
- PDF License with your details

Please allow us up to 48hrs to process your download link and license.
