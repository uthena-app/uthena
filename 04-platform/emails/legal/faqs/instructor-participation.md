We love getting more instructors on Uthena, so you are welcome to join.

We do have a few requirements in place:

- Your course consists of high-quality videos and materials
- You are the rightful owner of the course and can prove this
- You are allowing us to sell your course with PLR rights and are aware what these are

If that works for you, [start our partner onboarding](/partner/onboarding) and become the next instructor on Uthena! Want to refer people instead? See our [affiliate program](/affiliate/onboarding).

### How much do I receive?

We pay **60% of the net revenue** to our instructors. If your course is part of a bundle, the revenue is equally divided over the courses in there. So if there are 10 courses, and you have one of them in there — you will receive 10% of the sales price.

For the full instructor agreement (payments, schedules, course removal, etc.), see the [Terms of Service](/terms#uthena-instructor-terms).
