last_updated: 2026-06-29

Our Lifetime Access gives you access to the course. You do not get rights to alter or sell the courses yourself. You get access through our e-learning platform where you will be able to follow the course.

Lifetime stands for as long as that the course is available on our platform. Browse the [course catalog](/browse) to see what is available today.
