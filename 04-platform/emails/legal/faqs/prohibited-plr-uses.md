question: "What am I NOT allowed to do with a PLR course?"
last_updated: 2025-09-08
---

You are **not allowed** to:

- Use the course for YouTube or other free video sites
- Add the course to free membership sites
- Give the course away for free
- Use or sell the course on Uthena
- Use or sell the course on Udemy, Skillshare, or other course platforms you don't own
- Offer Resale Rights, Master Resale Rights, or Private Label Rights on the course

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, [contact us](/contact) before publishing.
