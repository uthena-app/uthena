last_updated: 2026-06-29

We offer a **14-day refund policy**, starting from the date of your purchase, for all video courses, PLR video courses, and MRR video courses on Uthena.

- All refund requests must be submitted directly through your Uthena account. Open the order on [your order history](https://uthena.com/account/orders) and click **Request a refund**.
- Refund requests made by email or other channels will not be accepted.
- Before filing your request, we encourage you to reach out via your account so we have the opportunity to resolve any issue to your satisfaction.

When your refund is issued:

- You will be unenrolled from the course(s), bundle(s), and bonuses you received.
- Any PLR/MRR license(s) will be revoked.
- You are required to delete all downloaded files and any copy of the PLR/MRR license issued.

Refunds are processed within 10 days from when we receive the cancellation. The full terms are on the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy) page. For how we handle your personal data during this process, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
