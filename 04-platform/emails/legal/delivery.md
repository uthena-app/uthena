# Delivery Policy

At Uthena, all of our products are **digital downloads and online course licenses**. Nothing we sell is shipped to you physically. Every order is fulfilled electronically and shows up in your account moments after payment is confirmed.

## Instant Digital Access

- **Instant access** — as soon as your payment is confirmed, your new product appears in your [library](/library). Access is typically available within **seconds**.
- You can log in to Uthena at any time to revisit your courses, downloads, and licenses from the same library page.
- Every order also generates a confirmation email so you have a record of the purchase; the email is a courtesy receipt — your library is always the source of truth.

## No Physical Shipment

- We do not ship any physical goods. There is **no physical delivery** of any kind for any Uthena product.
- Because nothing is shipped, there are **no shipping fees**, no handling fees, no customs duties, and no delivery delays to wait on.
- **No delivery address is required** at checkout. Your account email is the only contact needed to grant you access.

## International Availability

- Our digital downloads and licenses are available worldwide.
- All orders are fulfilled electronically, so you can access your purchase from anywhere with an internet connection.

## Refunds

- Because every product is delivered digitally the moment payment is confirmed, our refund window is short. See our full [Refund Policy](/refund-policy) for the current window, how to request a refund, the EU 14-day cooling-off period, and the non-refundable items list.
- If you are an EU buyer, note that downloading or accessing digital content acknowledges that your right of withdrawal is waived once the digital service has begun.

## Technical Issues and Support

If you experience any issues with delivery — for example, a product that does not appear in your library, a download link that fails, or a course that will not play — please reach out to our support team at [support@uthena.com](mailto:support@uthena.com). Include your order number and the email you used at checkout so we can locate your purchase quickly. We will respond as fast as we can to restore your access.
