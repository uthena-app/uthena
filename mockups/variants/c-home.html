§06 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Help center →

What is private label rights?

Private label rights (PLR) let you edit, rebrand, and resell a product as your own. You can change the instructor name, translate the content, add your own modules, and resell at any price. The product is yours to run your business on.

How much can I earn as a partner?

You earn sixty percent of every sale. The platform handles pricing, payment processing, refunds, and PLR distribution. Top partners earn $20k+ per month; the average is around $3,400.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Seven-day refund policy on all products, after any downloaded files are removed. Refund rate is below one percent across the catalogue.

Can I use Uthena courses on other platforms?

You can sell the course on any platform you own — your website, your own course platform, your agency offering. The license does not allow resale on Udemy, Skillshare, or other third-party course marketplaces.
