What this course is

A complete, end-to-end playbook for building a personal brand on social media using AI tools. The course covers positioning, content production, audience growth, monetisation, and the day-to-day operations of running a one-person media business.

What you'll get

  • 47 video lessons across 8 modules
  • The full transcript and slide decks
  • The content calendar template and prompt library
  • Private label rights — your name, your brand, your revenue

Who this is for

Digital entrepreneurs, course creators, affiliate marketers, and agencies who want to add personal-brand services or products to their offering. The course is structured so it can be sold as-is or repackaged into a higher-ticket offer with consulting.

What you can do with the license

The PLR license lets you rebrand and resell the course as your own. Translate it, change the instructor name, add your own modules, bundle it with coaching, or run it as part of a paid membership. The full rights agreement is on the License tab.
