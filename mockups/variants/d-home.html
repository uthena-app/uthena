§06 — Questions

Frequently
asked.

Everything you need to know about the marketplace, licensing, and the partner program. Still have a question? Hit the help center.

What is private label rights?

Private label rights (PLR) let you edit, rebrand, and resell a product as your own. You can change the instructor name, translate the content, add your own modules, and resell at any price.

How much can I earn as a partner?

You earn sixty percent of every sale. Top partners earn $20k+ per month; the average is around $3,400.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Seven-day refund policy on all products, after any downloaded files are removed. Refund rate is below one percent across the catalogue.

Can I use Uthena courses on other platforms?

You can sell on any platform you own. The license does not allow resale on Udemy, Skillshare, or other third-party course marketplaces.
