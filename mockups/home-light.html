Questions

Frequently asked

What is PLR and can I really keep 100% of the revenue?

Private Label Rights means you can rebrand, edit, and resell the course under your own name. You pay once, sell as many times as you like, and keep every dollar.

How is MRR different from PLR?

Master Resell Rights lets your buyers also resell the course. PLR is for you to sell; MRR is for you to build a reseller chain on top.

Can I edit the videos and course materials?

Yes. Source files, scripts, slide decks and videos are all included and editable in standard tools.

Do you offer refunds?

14-day return rights, no questions asked. Single courses are refundable if the course has not been downloaded.

What format are the courses delivered in?

MP4 video, PDF workbooks, slide decks, and editable project files where applicable.

Can I become an instructor?

Apply through the Earn with Uthena page. We license original work from independent creators and split revenue 70/30 in your favor.
