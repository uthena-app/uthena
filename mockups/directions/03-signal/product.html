Overview

Personal brands are the highest-leverage asset on the internet, and AI has collapsed the time it takes to build one. This course is a complete operating system for going from unknown to monetized: positioning, an AI-powered content engine, distribution and a monetization stack.

As a PLR product, it is also a business in a box. You get every video, slide deck and worksheet as editable source files — rebrand it, restructure it, translate it, and sell it through your own storefront at whatever price you choose.

What your students will learn

  • Position themselves in a crowded niche using AI research
  • Build a weekly content engine that runs on prompt templates
  • Repurpose one long-form idea into a week of short-form content
  • Grow an audience across platforms without burning out
  • Launch a first digital offer with AI-written sales copy
  • Scale to multiple income streams with a 12-month roadmap
