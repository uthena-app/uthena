Four design directions

Each direction covers the same three pages — home, category (AI Courses), product detail — with identical content and structure from the specs, so only the design varies. All dark, all professional.

Baseline for comparison: the current v3 mockups in mockups/ (teal, Linear-mood). These four deliberately explore different territory.

/01

Ledger

The operator's financial terminal. Blue-black canvas, emerald accent, IBM Plex Mono on every number, ruled lines and ledger motifs. Sells the "keep 100% of revenue" story by looking like money infrastructure.

Mood: Stripe Dashboard × trading terminal · Inter + IBM Plex Mono
HomeCategoryProduct
/02

Atelier

The premium licensing house. Warm charcoal, Fraunces serif display, brass-gold accent, numbered editorial sections, gallery-style cards. Positions the catalog as a curated collection — justifies premium pricing.

Mood: fine licensing house / type foundry · Fraunces + Inter
HomeCategoryProduct
/03

Signal

The modern SaaS launch. Deep indigo canvas, electric violet with a single cyan gradient pairing, Space Grotesk headlines, restrained glow on hero and final CTA only. The most "2026 startup" of the four.

Mood: Linear / Raycast / Frame.io · Space Grotesk + Inter + JetBrains Mono
HomeCategoryProduct
/04

Forge

The industrial catalog. Graphite canvas, visible ruled grid with crosshair marks, zero-radius collapsed-border cards with index numbers, condensed Archivo headlines, single orange accent — a nod to the logo. Inventory for serious operators.

Mood: Vercel × industrial parts catalog · Archivo + Inter + IBM Plex Mono
